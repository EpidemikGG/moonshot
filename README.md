# 🚀 MOONSHOT — Search-Volume Sky Trivia

**MOONSHOT** is a 10-round sky-to-moon trivia browser game where the goal isn't just to give a correct answer, but to give a **1 or 2 word Keyword** with the **lowest monthly Google search volume** compared to the question's monthly average.

## 🌕 Gameplay & Scoring Tiers

Each round presents a question and its **Average Monthly Google Search Volume** across valid keywords. You have **30 seconds** to submit a valid **1 or 2 word Keyword**:

| Tier | Points | Rarity Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| 💎 **Diamond** | **50 pts** | Ultra-niche monthly Google search that answers the question (almost impossible!) |
| 💠 **Platinum** | **40 pts** | Still very rare monthly Google search |
| 🥇 **Gold** | **30 pts** | Rare — searched less that month than the question's average |
| 🥈 **Silver** | **20 pts** | Common — searched around the question's monthly average |
| 🥉 **Bronze** | **10 pts** | Very common — heavy monthly Google search volume |

Across **10 rounds**, a perfect **500 points** propels your spacecraft from Earth's launchpad (`0 km`) through the Troposphere, Stratosphere, Kármán Line, and Deep Space all the way to **The Moon (`384,400 km`)**!

## 🛠️ Modes
- **📅 Daily Launch**: Same 10 questions for everyone each day, with a shareable emoji telemetry log.
- **♾️ Free Play**: Endless randomized 10-round missions from a 25-topic curated dataset.
