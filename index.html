🚀 How to Play MOONSHOT

Inspired by rarity-first trivia, MOONSHOT challenges you to ascend from Earth's launchpad all the way to The Moon (500 points / 384,400 km) across 10 rounds.

  • 1 or 2 Word Keywords: Every answer you submit must be 1 or 2 words max.
  • Monthly Google Search Scoring: Each question has a benchmark Average Monthly Google Search Volume across valid answers. If your valid keyword was searched less that month than the average, you earn higher tier points!
  • 5 Rarity Tiers:
    • 💎 Diamond (50 pts): Ultra-niche Google search that month (almost impossible!)
    • 💠 Platinum (40 pts): Still very rare monthly search volume
    • 🥇 Gold (30 pts): Rare — searched less than the monthly question average
    • 🥈 Silver (20 pts): Common — around the monthly question average
    • 🥉 Bronze (10 pts): Very common — mainstream, high-volume Google search
  • 30-Second Stage Timer: Unrecognized or >2-word guesses let you keep trying until the 30s clock runs out!
